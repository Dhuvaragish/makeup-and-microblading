Clinical Methodology

Wet Healing vs. Dry Healing Protocols

Why one size does not fit all in permanent makeup. How our clinical protocols adapt to your unique epidermal histology.

Recommended for 90% of Clients VELOURA Standard Protocol

The VELOURA Wet Healing Method

Modern medical dermatology shows that controlled moist wound healing accelerates epidermal cellular migration by up to 40% compared to dry scab formation, resulting in significantly crispier microblading retention.

Who This Is Prescribed For:
  • Normal, dry, mature, sensitive, and combination skin types
  • Clients receiving Microblading, Nano Brows, or Velvet Lip Blush
  • Clients living in dry, low-humidity, or air-conditioned environments
The 3-Step Daily Routine (Days 2 to 10):
  1. Gentle Cleanse: Lather antibacterial foam wash with fingertips for 10 seconds; rinse with lukewarm distilled water.
  2. Pat Bone-Dry: Gently press with a single-use sterile paper towel. Never wipe or rub.
  3. Microscopic Barrier Veil: Apply a rice-grain amount (split between both brows) of VELOURA balm. Skin must look matte, never shiny or greasy.
85–95% Pigment Retention Minimal Thick Crusting
Specialized Skin Protocol Sebaceous Control

The Controlled Dry Healing Method

When sebaceous glands produce excess sebum, applying supplemental lipid ointments can suffocate dermal pores, trap bacteria, and cause micro-pigment particles to blur or leach outward.

Who This Is Prescribed For:
  • Hyper-oily skin, large sebaceous pores, active T-zone sheen
  • Clients receiving Saline Pigment Lightening or Removal
  • Clients prone to oily cystic breakouts near the brow line
The 3-Step Daily Routine (Days 1 to 7):
  1. Sebum Blotting: Press clean oil-blotting paper over brows every 2–3 hours to remove natural skin oils without water.
  2. Water Restriction: Keep completely dry for first 72 hours. From Day 4, rinse briefly with cool water only.
  3. Zero Ointment: Do not apply balm unless extreme painful tightness occurs, in which case apply a microscopic dot once daily.
Prevents Stroke Blurring Pure Sebaceous Control
Milestone Guidelines

Lifestyle & Activity Restriction Matrix

Exact return-to-activity timelines for working out, swimming, cosmetic makeup, sunshine, and active skincare ingredients.

First 48 Hours
No Alcohol, Caffeine & Thinners

Avoid wine, cocktails, espresso, and blood-thinning NSAIDs (aspirin, ibuprofen). They dilate capillaries and cause blood and lymph seepage that forces pigment out of the channels.

Sleep Protocol: Sleep strictly on your back on a clean silk pillowcase to avoid friction.
Days 1 to 7
Zero Sweaty Gym Workouts

No cardio, weightlifting, spin class, hot yoga, or heavy physical exertion. Sweat contains sodium chloride and urea, which act like natural saline and chemically leach fresh pigment.

Permitted: Gentle casual walking in cool indoor air-conditioned spaces is fine.
Days 1 to 10
No Direct Shower Spray or Steam

Do not let hot high-pressure shower jets hit your face. No saunas, steam rooms, Jacuzzis, or long hot baths. Prolonged hot steam softens healing crusts prematurely.

Pro Tip: Use the peel-and-stick forehead shower shields included in your VELOURA Recovery Kit.
Days 1 to 14
Zero Cosmetics on Treated Area

No foundation, BB cream, concealer, brow pomade, brow pencil, or lip gloss directly on or within 1 inch of the procedure zone. Makeup harbors bacteria that cause cellular infections.

Safe Zone: Mascara and eyeshadow on upper lid only after eyeliner crusts shed.
Days 1 to 30
No Sunbathing or Tanning Beds

Direct ultraviolet radiation fractures organic and inorganic cosmetic pigment bonds, causing severe fading or premature color shifts. Wear wide-brimmed hats outdoors.

After Day 14: Apply mineral SPF 50 (Zinc Oxide or Titanium Dioxide) every morning.
Days 1 to 45
No Retinol, Acids or Injections

Strictly avoid Retin-A, Tretinoin, Glycolic, Lactic, Salicylic Acids, and Vitamin C within 2 inches. Postpone Botox, Dysport, dermal fillers, microneedling, and chemical peels until week 6.

Reason: Active acids accelerate epidermal turnover and peel cosmetic pigment prematurely.
Clinical Rules

The Golden Dos & Don'ts

The foundational commandments every client must honor to protect their permanent makeup investment.

Essential Dos: What You MUST Do

  • Dab lymph fluid faithfully on Day 1: Blot with clean sterile cotton rounds every 30–60 mins to prevent thick scab buildup.
  • Wash hands thoroughly before touching your face: Always sanitize hands with antibacterial soap before applying aftercare balm.
  • Use single-use cotton swabs: Dispense balm using clean sterile Q-tips to prevent container bacterial contamination.
  • Sleep elevated on your back: Prop head on two pillows with a fresh satin/silk pillowcase to prevent friction and nocturnal pillow rubbing.
  • Drink plenty of water: Internal systemic hydration accelerates deep epidermal keratinization and tissue remodeling.
  • Apply Mineral SPF 50 after Day 14: Daily physical zinc oxide sunscreen is the #1 defense against long-term pigment discoloration.
  • Honor the 6-to-8 week touch-up window: The perfecting session locks in density, refines color nuance, and secures 24-month retention.

Strict Don'ts: What You MUST AVOID

  • DO NOT pick, scratch, peel, or rub flakes: Pulling off a flake will drag the underlying pigment out of the dermis, leaving permanent bald spots or scar tissue.
  • NO sweating, heavy gym workouts, or spin classes for 7–10 days: Salt crystals in sweat dissolve fresh pigment before it bonds to dermal collagen.
  • NO high-pressure shower spray directly on the face for 10 days: Keep head tilted back while washing hair or use our adhesive shower visors.
  • NO makeup, foundation, tinted creams, or powders on the tattoo for 14 days: Traps bacteria and causes inflammatory foreign-body reactions.
  • NO anti-aging skincare acids (Retinol, Glycolic, Salicylic, AHA/BHA): Maintain a strict 1-inch exclusion zone around brows and lips indefinitely.
  • NO swimming in ocean water, chlorinated pools, or soaking in hot tubs for 14 days: Harsh chemicals and waterborne bacteria pose serious infection risks.
  • NO sleeping on your stomach or sides during week 1: Friction against pillow fabric pulls off micro-crusts while you are asleep.

How your unique skin biology, sebaceous activity, and melanin concentration influence healed permanent makeup results.

Cell turnover is slower (taking 6–8 weeks). Flaking takes 10–12 days to begin. However, healed microblading strokes remain exceptionally crisp, clean, and defined.

Recommendation: Strict VELOURA Wet Healing protocol with twice-daily micro-balm application to prevent fragile skin cracking.
Fitzpatrick IV – VI
Melanin-Rich Skin

Higher eumelanin concentration requires custom warm-undertone pigments to prevent healed ashiness. Lip blush may exhibit temporary hyperpigmentation before blooming.

Recommendation: Two-phase warm neutralization protocol. Daily mineral SPF 50 is strictly non-negotiable to prevent darkening.
Reactive / Rosacea
Sensitive & Fair Skin

May experience mild localized erythema (pinkness) or a histamine reaction for 24–48 hours post-procedure. Capillaries sit closer to the stratum lucidum.

Recommendation: Clean cold gel compress therapy. Avoid all fragranced facial products and sleep elevated on 2 pillows.
Emergency Protocol Clinical Step-by-Step Guidance

What To Do If a Scab or Flake Is Accidentally Knocked Off Early

If a flake catches on a sweater, gets scratched in your sleep, or peels off accidentally in the shower, do not panic. Follow this 4-step emergency triage protocol to minimize pigment loss and avoid scarring:

Step 1: STOP & SNIP

DO NOT pull the rest! If a loose flake is dangling, carefully trim the detached edge with sanitized cosmetic scissors. Never pull or rip it.

Step 2: SANITIZE

Press a sterile saline-soaked gauze pad gently over the exposed spot for 60 seconds. Do not rub. This disinfects and calms capillary bleeding.

Step 3: SEAL

Apply a pinpoint microscopic dab of VELOURA healing ointment directly onto the exposed area to recreate an artificial sterile lipid shield.

Step 4: NOTIFY

Photograph the spot in natural daylight and send it to our WhatsApp concierge. We will note the area to restore during your 6-week touch-up.

Longevity Guide

Long-Term Pigment Preservation Manual

How to protect your permanent makeup investment and extend your results up to 24+ months.

Daily Mineral SPF 50

UVA and UVB photons shatter cosmetic pigment molecular bonds. Apply physical zinc oxide or titanium dioxide sunscreen directly over healed brows and lip borders every morning without fail.

1-Inch Exfoliant Buffer Zone

Retinol, Tretinoin, Glycolic Acid, Salicylic Acid, and Vitamin C accelerate epidermal renewal. Keep all active anti-aging serums and scrubs at least 1 inch away from your brows and lips indefinitely.

Laser & Chemical Shielding

During IPL, Fraxel, PicoSure, or medical peels, explicitly instruct your dermatologist or laser technician to shield your cosmetic tattoos. Laser pulses can turn cosmetic pigments permanently black or charcoal gray.
