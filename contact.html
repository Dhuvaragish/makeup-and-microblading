The Atelier Experience

What to Expect on Your Visit Day

From the moment you step out of the private elevator onto Madison Avenue, your comfort, safety, and aesthetic vision guide every stage of our clinical protocol.

STAGE 01
Arrival & Welcome Ritual

Check in with our concierge 10 minutes prior to your session. Enjoy a warm botanical herbal infusion or sparkling mineral water in our tranquil lounge while reviewing your digital health intake and Fitzpatrick profile.

STAGE 02
Golden Ratio Facial Mapping

Your master artist conducts a 45-minute bespoke pre-drawing session using surgical calipers and bone structure analysis. Nothing begins until you personally review, approve, and love the hand-drawn pencil outline.

STAGE 03
Dual-Stage Topical Anesthesia

We apply pharmaceutical-grade 5% lidocaine and prilocaine topical numbing cream for 25 minutes prior to and continuously during treatment, ensuring a calm, painless, and serene experience. Most clients drift off to sleep.

STAGE 04
The Reveal & Aftercare Kit

Admire your fresh enhancement under 5000K daylight photography rings. We provide an unboxing of your complimentary Clinical Recovery Kit (medical balm, sterile cleansing wipes, barrier shield, and instruction card).

Client Safety & Eligibility

Pre-Visit Health Quick-Check

To ensure safe healing, optimal pigment retention, and immaculate results, please review these key timing guidelines before your appointment.

Accutane & Retinoids

You must be completely off oral Accutane (Isotretinoin) for a minimum of 12 months. Discontinue topical Retin-A / retinol around brows 14 days prior.

Botox & Fillers

Please schedule any forehead/brow Botox at least 3 to 4 weeks before or wait 3 weeks after, ensuring facial muscles have completely settled into place.

Alcohol & Caffeine

Avoid alcohol, coffee, energy drinks, and blood thinners (aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil) for 24 to 48 hours prior to prevent excess capillary weeping.

Nursing & Pregnancy

Permanent makeup is medically contraindicated during pregnancy and while actively nursing. We look forward to welcoming you after nursing concludes.

Studio Protocol

Studio Policies & Client Etiquette

To maintain an impeccably sterile, relaxed, and punctual environment for all guests, please review our studio guidelines.

15-Min Punctuality Window

Please arrive 10 minutes early to complete topical numbing paperwork. Arrivals later than 15 minutes may require rescheduling to preserve sterile procedural time.

Sterile Suite Solo Policy

Under NYC Department of Health regulations, no guests, children, or pets may enter sterile procedure suites. Companions are warmly invited to relax in our lounge.

Makeup-Free Skin

Please arrive without foundation, brow makeup, or lip tint in the intended treatment zone. If you wear everyday brow pencil, wear it so we can observe your daily style.

48-Hour Rescheduling

Deposits are 100% transferable to any date within 6 months when requested at least 48 hours in advance. No-shows or same-day cancellations forfeit the booking deposit.
