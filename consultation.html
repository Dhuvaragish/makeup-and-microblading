Medical Contraindications & Eligibility Guide

Your safety is paramount. Please review our clinical guidelines prior to submitting your assessment or scheduling an appointment.

Absolute Contraindications

Under medical board and NYC health guidelines, we cannot perform permanent makeup on clients who:

  • Are currently pregnant or breastfeeding (hormonal fluctuations cause pigment rejection and anesthetic risks).
  • Have taken Accutane (Isotretinoin) within the past 12 months (skin barrier requires 1 full year to rebuild).
  • Have a history of keloid scar formation or hypertrophic scarring.
  • Are undergoing active chemotherapy or radiation (must be 6 months post-treatment with oncological clearance).
  • Have an active skin infection, eczema, or psoriasis in the brow or lip treatment area.

Temporary / Conditional Restrictions

Procedures can be safely scheduled once the following waiting periods have elapsed:

  • Botox or Dysport: Wait at least 14 days after injections so muscle posture has stabilized.
  • Dermal Lip Fillers: Wait at least 4 weeks before or after Velvet Lip Blush for optimal symmetry.
  • Retin-A / Tretinoin / Chemical Peels: Discontinue 14 days prior to your appointment.
  • History of Cold Sores (For Lips): Take doctor-prescribed antiviral (Valtrex) 3 days prior and 3 days post-treatment.
  • Recent Sun Tan or Sunburn: Skin must be fully healed and at baseline natural tone.
Colorimetry Mastery

Fitzpatrick Scale & Melanin Color Harmony

We customize our pigment chemistry across all Fitzpatrick phototypes I through VI. For cool undertones, we infuse warm golden modifiers to prevent ashy gray tones. For hyperpigmented melanin lips, we apply targeted warm orange/coral neutralizing pigments before depositing sheer nude-rose color.
