The Founder's Story

"Permanent makeup should celebrate your bone structure, never overtake it."

Founded by PhiBrows Grand Master Elena Rostova, VELOURA Studio was born out of frustration with harsh, cookie-cutter eyebrow tattoos that dominated the industry. Having trained in clinical dermatology in Munich and cosmetic tattooing in Vienna, Elena set out to create an atelier dedicated purely to hyper-realism.

Today, our Madison Avenue clinic is home to 4 world-renowned artists who have performed over 6,500 procedures. We utilize organic, vegan pigments that age true-to-tone without morphing into gray or red shades.

The Atelier Standard

Our 6-Step Facial Harmony Protocol

We never rush art. Every procedure follows our rigorous clinical pathway designed for flawless symmetry and longevity.

Phase 01
Cranial Morphology & Mapping

We measure facial golden ratio proportions (1:1.618) relative to your bone structure, nasal bridge, and ocular distance—never using generic stencils.

Phase 02
Custom Pigment Colorimetry

We analyze your skin's undertone (cool, neutral, warm, or olive) to mix a bespoke formula that heals true-to-tone and never oxidizes gray or salmon pink.

Phase 03
Dual-Stage Topical Anesthesia

We apply prescription-strength 5% lidocaine pre-numbing cream, followed by a secondary epinephrine micro-gel during treatment for a virtually pain-free experience.

Phase 04
Sub-Millimeter Implantation

Using medical-grade 0.18mm nano-blades or single-needle digital acoustic motors, we implant pigment precisely at the basal membrane junction (upper dermis).

Phase 05
Clinical Recovery Package

You leave with our complete take-home medical aftercare kit including sterile distilled saline wipes, breathable barrier gel, and day-by-day healing roadmap.

Phase 06
Week 6 Refinement Touch-Up

At 6-8 weeks, we inspect healed retention, reinforce strokes in lower density areas, and perfect stroke crispness for 18 to 24 month lasting beauty.

Formulation Science

Why Our Pigments Never Turn Red, Blue, or Gray

Traditional body tattoo inks contain high concentrations of coarse heavy metal oxides that migrate under the dermis and discolor into unnatural green or blue tones over time.

At VELOURA Atelier, we formulate exclusively with next-generation organic and hybrid micro-pigment blends certified under stringent EU REACH 2022 Regulations. Our pigments feature lightfast, gamma-sterilized molecules that break down uniformly through natural skin cell turnover, fading down gracefully in tone without shifting hue.
