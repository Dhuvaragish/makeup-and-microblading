Healing Roadmap

The 30-Day Lip Blush Journey

Understanding what to expect prevents post-procedure anxiety. Trust the process!

Days 1 - 2
Bold & Pouty

Lips appear 40% darker than healed result with slight plumping swelling. Color looks like intense matte lipstick.

Days 3 - 5
The Peeling Phase

Lips become dry and begin flaking like winter chapping. Never pick! Apply aftercare balm 5-6 times daily.

Days 7 - 14
The "Ghost" Phase

New epithelial skin masks pigment. Lips may look like color vanished! Do not panic—pigment is settling below the surface.

Days 28 - 42
Blooming Perfection

Color re-emerges with full translucence and softness. Ready for your 6-8 week perfecting touch-up session.

Healing Journey

The 3 Phases of Healed Lip Blush

What to anticipate as your delicate lip mucosa regenerates into a sheer, healthy watercolor flush.

Days 1 - 2
Plump & Vibrant

Lips appear 40% darker and slightly plumped like freshly applied matte lipstick. Blot away lymph fluid gently with distilled water pads.

Days 3 - 5
Chapping & Micro-Shedding

Lips will feel tight and begin shedding a fine outer layer. Keep lips hydrated with our studio balm every 2 hours. Do not pick or peel flakes.

Weeks 4 - 6
The Healed Watercolor Bloom

The deeper skin layers renew, revealing an effortless translucent tinted balm flush that lasts 2 to 4 years without smudging or lipstick transfer.
