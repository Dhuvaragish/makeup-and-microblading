Fresh Day-1 vs Healed 6-Week Results

Understanding the biological healing shift helps you anticipate every stage of your transformation with total confidence.

Phase 1
Day 1: Sharp & Saturated

Pigment rests in both the epidermis and upper dermis. Strokes appear sharp, dark, and slightly elevated. Skin may exhibit subtle rosy undertones around mapped boundaries.

Phase 2
Days 7-14: Flaking & Ghosting

As the top stratum corneum layer naturally sloughs off, strokes temporarily appear milky or faded. This is completely normal as new collagen-rich skin covers the pigment.

Phase 3
Weeks 4-6: True Color Bloom

The epidermis fully settles and becomes translucent. The true pigment color blooms to the surface, appearing 30-40% softer, 10-15% lighter, and perfectly integrated into real hair.
