Client Preparation

Pre-Care Preparation Protocol

Proper preparation ensures minimum bleeding, maximum pigment retention, and optimal comfort during your appointment.

2 Weeks Prior
Skin Preparations
  • No Botox or Fillers: Avoid neurotoxin or dermal filler injections in forehead or lip area for 14 days prior.
  • No Chemical Peels / Laser: Discontinue AHA/BHA, retinol, tretinoin, and laser facial treatments.
  • No Tanning or Sunburns: Skin must be calm and uncompromised on appointment day.
48 Hours Prior
Blood Thinners
  • No Alcohol: Avoid wine, beer, and spirits for 48 hours to prevent excess capillary bleeding.
  • No NSAIDs or Aspirin: Avoid ibuprofen, Advil, Aleve, or high-dose Vitamin E / Fish Oil supplements.
  • Cold Sore Precaution: If getting Lip Blush and prone to cold sores, take doctor-prescribed antiviral Valtrex 3 days prior.
Day Of Appointment
Arrival Guidelines
  • No Caffeine: Avoid morning coffee, matcha, and energy drinks to keep heart rate and blood pressure calm.
  • No Heavy Workouts: Avoid strenuous cardiovascular workouts right before treatment.
  • Eat a Nourishing Meal: Ensure your blood sugar is stable before your session; arrive well-hydrated.
