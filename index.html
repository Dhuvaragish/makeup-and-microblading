Our Methodology

The Aura 5-Step Journey

We take the anxiety out of semi-permanent makeup with complete transparency, pain-free numbing, and client approval before any blading begins.

1
Facial Mapping

We measure your bone structure using calipers and the Golden Ratio, creating a custom symmetry outline.

2
Pigment Matching

We swatch pigments directly on your forehead or jawline to formulate the exact undertone complement.

3
Dual Numbing

Medical-grade topical lidocaine numbing cream ensures a virtually painless, relaxing spa experience.

4
Precision Work

Using 0.18mm disposable blades or nano-needles, micro-strokes are deposited with millimeter precision.

5
6-Week Touch-Up

Once your skin heals, we refine any subtle spots to lock in lasting pigment retention and vibrancy.
