Our Methodology

1
Facial Mapping

We measure your bone structure using calipers and the Golden Ratio, creating a custom symmetry outline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparation, procedural comfort, and long-term pigment longevity at VELOURA.

Semi-permanent cosmetic tattooing typically maintains optimal vibrancy for 18 to 36 months, depending on skin metabolism, lifestyle, sun exposure, and daily skincare. Signature Microblading on normal-to-dry skin retains crisp definition for 18–24 months, while Ombré Powder brows and Velvet Lip Blush often last 2 to 3+ years. We recommend a complimentary 12-to-18-month color refresher to maintain sharp lines and warm radiance.

Client comfort is our highest priority. We use a dual-stage medical topical numbing protocol: a 5% lidocaine pre-numbing formulation applied for 25 minutes prior to blading, followed by a secondary lidocaine-epinephrine gel during the session. Over 95% of our clients rate the discomfort a 1 or 2 out of 5—describing it as a light electric toothbrush vibration or mild scratching. Many of our clients actually relax and fall asleep.

Microblading utilizes a hand-held ultra-fine blade to sculpt crisp, individual 3D hair strokes into the upper dermal layer—ideal for clients with normal-to-dry skin desiring an undetectable, natural enhancement. Ombré Powder Brows uses a specialized digital machine to deposit thousands of microscopic pigment pixels, creating a soft, misty gradient finish that mimics filled-in cosmetic powder. Ombré is universally suited for all skin types, including oily, active, and mature skin.

During Days 1–3, pigment appears 20–30% darker and bolder than your final healed result as skin oxidizes. By Days 4–7, light micro-flaking occurs as the upper stratum corneum exfoliates; apply a rice-grain veil of your complimentary VELOURA healing ointment and avoid picking. By Week 2, pigment softens significantly during the natural collagen "ghosting" phase, before blooming into its true, soft hue around Weeks 4–6.

Without exception. VELOURA formulates exclusively with next-generation organic and hybrid mineral pigments certified under strict EU REACH 2022 safety directives. Every batch is gamma-ray sterilized, 100% vegan, cruelty-free, and formulated without nickel, iron oxides, or heavy metal carriers. Our pigments fade down gracefully true-to-tone without morphing into unnatural blue, red, or gray undertones.
