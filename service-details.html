The Science Behind The Art

Artistry & Clinical Method

Our signature micropigmentation method utilizes medical-grade equipment and biocompatible vegan pigments implanted into the upper dermis.

Cellular Micro-Encapsulation Guarantee

Unlike traditional deep body tattoos that enter the reticular dermis and blur over time, VELOURA pigments are micro-implanted strictly into the upper papillary layer (0.08mm to 0.15mm depth). This maintains razor-sharp definition, soft edges, and allows natural fading without unnatural gray, blue, or red discolouration.

Biological Recovery Timeline

Day-by-Day Post-Care & Healing Roadmap

Permanent makeup is a living biological process. Understand what to expect throughout the 6-week skin turnover cycle:

Days 1 - 2
Bold, Defined & Slightly Tender

Pigment oxidizes upon initial air exposure, appearing 20-30% darker than the target healed tone. Blot gently with sterile damp cotton pads every 2 hours to prevent excess lymph crust.

Days 3 - 5
Micro-Flaking & Epidermal Shedding

Skin begins natural cellular turnover with light flaking. Rule #1: NEVER pick, scrub, or scratch. Apply a microscopic film of our post-care botanical peptide balm twice daily.

Days 6 - 10
The "Ghosting" Phenomenon

As flakes detach, the pigment may appear temporarily milky, pale, or "missing." Do not panic! A fresh stratum corneum layer has formed over the pigment, temporarily obscuring its depth.

Weeks 4 - 6
True Pigment Bloom & Touch-Up

Internal cellular renewal completes, and your true rich color blooms to the surface. Your 6-8 week perfecting session reinforces retention and locks in results for 18-36 months.

Pre-Treatment Checklist (Preparation)

To ensure optimal pigment retention, prevent excess bleeding, and maximize comfort, please adhere to these preparation rules:

24 Hours Prior Avoid alcohol, coffee, energy drinks, green tea, aspirin, and ibuprofen. These thin the blood and dilute pigment uptake.
14 Days Prior Discontinue retinol, AHA/BHA chemical exfoliants, and salon peels. Avoid Botox or dermal fillers in the brow or forehead zone.
30 Days Prior Avoid laser skin resurfacing, intensive sun tanning, or sunburns. Skin must be healthy, calm, and fully intact.
Ideal Candidates
  • Desire natural, effortless daytime brow definition without daily makeup pencil.
  • Experience sparse hair growth from over-plucking, aging, or hormonal shifts.
  • Suffer from brow asymmetry or uneven arch height.
  • Active lifestyle: athletes, swimmers, and busy professionals who want smudge-proof perfection.
When to Choose An Alternative
  • Oily / Large Pores: Consider Ombré Powder or Digital Nano Brows for crisper healed longevity.
  • Covering Old Dark PMU: Color shift correction or powder shading may be required first.
  • Pregnant / Nursing: Must wait until breastfeeding is completed.

Essential Second Phase

The 6-8 Week Perfecting Session

Permanent makeup is an essential two-phase artistic process. During healing, human skin naturally absorbs and sheds 20-30% of implanted pigment. Your perfecting session allows your master artist to:

Reinforce strokes in lower-retention skin areas.
Micro-adjust undertone warmth if skin cooled the tone.
Enhance tail sharpness, arch density, and border crispness.
Lock in 18 to 36 month pigment durability.
