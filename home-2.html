The 4 Master Brow Modalities

We master all four distinct permanent makeup modalities so you receive the exact right procedure for your skin.

Technique 01
3D Featherstrokes

Implanted by hand blade in flowing curves that mimic genuine hair direction.

Best for: Normal to dry skin
Technique 02
Ombré Pixel Mist

Machine dot work creating soft powdery gradient with zero skin slicing.

Best for: Oily or mature skin
Technique 03
Hybrid Combo

Microbladed hair strokes at the head blending into soft powder shading at the tail.

Best for: Sparse or patchy arches
Technique 04
Digital Nano Strokes

Ultra-fine single acoustic needle depositing microscopic pigment beads.

Best for: Fragile or rosacea-prone skin
